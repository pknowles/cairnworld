# The Four Brothers

A short investigative scenario for Cairn, adapted from Borderlands 2's Won't Get Fooled Again.

## Premise

A young man, Jory Macready, lies dead in the village square. Four nearly identical brothers have been detained nearby.

The local marshal knows one of them murdered Jory, but cannot distinguish them reliably. He asks the adventurers to investigate, threatening to hang all four if nobody identifies the killer.

The central puzzle is to identify the one brother who satisfies three independent pieces of evidence. This preserves the structure of the original mission.

## The characters

| Character        | Role            | Personality and information                                                                                                                   |
| ---------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Marshal Friedman | Local lawman    | Impatient, lazy, casually brutal. Heard the attack but ignored it until someone fetched him.                                                  |
| Madam Moxie      | Tavern keeper   | Proud, confident, dangerous. One brother demanded sanctuary, threatened her, and she wounded him.                                             |
| Old Zed          | Village surgeon | Competent but unscrupulous. Recently extracted a projectile from one brother and healed him. Nervous about the suspiciously generous payment. |
| Jory Macready    | Murder victim   | A young man killed by a single projectile to the throat.                                                                                      |

The four identical brothers, all of whom are unpleasant and uncooperative:

| Brother | Weapon                        | Protective charm | Condition |
| ------- | ----------------------------- | ---------------- | --------- |
| Hark    | Scattergun (multiple pellets) | Yes              | Wounded   |
| Ennis   | Hand crossbow                 | No               | Uninjured |
| Barlo   | Longbow                       | Yes              | Uninjured |
| Shin    | Explosive firepot             | Yes              | Wounded   |

The four brothers' equipment is deliberately arranged so that only Barlo satisfies all three clues.

## The evidence

1\. Marshal Friedman — The weapon

Jory was struck in the throat by a single, narrow projectile. The marshal also saw one of the brothers fleeing.

Deduction: The scattergun and firepot are inconsistent with the wound. A bow or crossbow could have caused it.

2\. Madam Moxie — The protection

One brother demanded protection in her tavern. When he became threatening, she fired three shots at him with her enchanted pistol.

His protective charm absorbed the first two shots. The third struck him.

Deduction: The culprit possesses a protective charm. This eliminates Ennis.

3\. Old Zed — The injury

He recently removed a pistol ball from one of the brothers and treated the wound completely.

The patient overpaid him, which Zed is reluctant to disclose.

Deduction: The culprit was shot but no longer bears an untreated wound. This distinguishes Barlo from the other protected, wounded suspects.

## The brothers' behaviour

Retaining their characterisation from the original transcript:

- Hark: Aggressive and openly contemptuous of the marshal. Insists the authorities have nothing on them.
- Ennis: Eager to blame his brothers, initially accusing Hark, then Shin. Wants to return to his work.
- Barlo: Defiant and sarcastic. Responds to accusations with insults rather than explanations.
- Shin: Callous about the victim, claiming Jory deserved his fate. Refuses to cooperate.

## Resolution

Correct accusation: Barlo admits the murder without remorse and threatens the investigators. Friedman immediately attempts to execute him.

Incorrect accusation: Barlo laughs, reveals that the party is wrong, and attempts to escape during the confusion. The players have a chance to stop him.

This retains the original game's outcome without requiring the players to resolve the situation through combat.

## Investigation requirements

The players must establish:

1. The murder weapon fired a single, narrow projectile.
2. The murderer possessed magical protection capable of absorbing two pistol shots.
3. The murderer subsequently received medical treatment and is now fully healed.
4. Only one brother matches all three conditions.

One important adaptation issue: In Borderlands, health and shield status are visible through the game interface. Cairn has no equivalent. For the mystery to work fairly, the brothers' protective charms and physical conditions must be observable or discoverable through ordinary inspection, without requiring players to guess what to examine.

The essential design is otherwise unchanged: four nearly indistinguishable suspects, three independent witnesses, three intersecting constraints, one culprit, with the possibility of a mistaken accusation and escape.
